# Assignment 2 – Data Management Competency Appraisal

## Areas of Confidence and Proficiency

My Data Management classes have provided a foundation in relational database design, data modeling, database implementation, SQL, generative artificial intelligence, prompt engineering, and AI-assisted research. These competencies have taught me to view data management as more than storing information. It involves organizing, validating, retrieving, protecting, and using data to support business operations, analysis, automation, and informed decision-making.

One of my strongest competencies is translating business requirements into organized data structures. I can examine business rules, identify the people, activities, transactions, and information involved, and determine how they should be represented within a database. I can identify entities, attributes, relationships, cardinalities, primary keys, and foreign keys and represent them using Entity-Relationship (ER) and Enhanced Entity-Relationship (EER) diagrams. Using Draw.io, I have developed conceptual database models and translated them into relational schemas.

I am also confident in relational database normalization through Third Normal Form (3NF). I understand how normalization reduces unnecessary duplication and prevents insertion, update, and deletion anomalies. Given a business scenario, I can separate information into related tables while preserving connections through primary and foreign keys.

Another area of proficiency is relational database implementation using MySQL. I can create tables using Data Definition Language (DDL), populate and modify them using Data Manipulation Language (DML), and retrieve information using SQL. These techniques allow me to transform stored records into information that answers practical business questions.

My experience also includes generative AI and AI-assisted information processing. I understand how to structure effective prompts using elements such as role, context, task, audience, constraints, source information, and expected output. I can translate a business problem into clear AI instructions, refine prompts when the initial output is inadequate, and evaluate generated content for accuracy, relevance, completeness, and appropriateness.

I am particularly confident in developing reusable prompts and AI-assisted workflows for research, analysis, summarization, comparison, document development, and information synthesis. Together, these competencies allow me to manage a process that progresses from business requirements and data modeling to database implementation, information retrieval, and AI-assisted analysis.

## Areas Requiring Further Development

Although I possess a strong foundation, several areas require additional practice. One is advanced SQL. I can develop joins, aggregate queries, and basic subqueries, but I am less comfortable constructing deeply nested subqueries, stored procedures, or highly complex SQL statements without examples or documentation.

My experience with MongoDB and other NoSQL databases is less extensive than my experience with relational databases. I would currently need guidance to design and query a sophisticated NoSQL solution independently.

Machine-learning implementation is another area requiring further practice. I understand foundational concepts related to supervised learning, classification, regression, and neural networks, but I am not yet proficient in training advanced models from scratch. I also have limited experience deploying and monitoring machine-learning models in production environments. These areas represent opportunities for continued growth rather than weaknesses in my foundational preparation.

## Desired Knowledge and Skills

I would like to strengthen my understanding of how traditional database systems connect with modern AI applications. Specifically, I want to learn how AI-enabled systems securely retrieve organizational data through application programming interfaces (APIs) and data pipelines and use that information to generate summaries, recommendations, predictions, or other decision-support outputs.

I also want to develop stronger competencies in data and AI governance. As organizations increasingly use AI to process business information, issues such as ownership, privacy, security, bias, accuracy, retention, accountability, and appropriate use become more important. Additional experience designing governance procedures that address both traditional databases and AI systems would improve my professional capabilities.

Finally, I want to improve my ability to evaluate AI-generated information. Effective AI use requires more than writing prompts; it also requires verifying claims, identifying hallucinations and bias, evaluating source quality, protecting confidential information, and determining when human review is necessary.

## Evidence of Competency

My **Online Food Ordering System** database project provides strong evidence of my Data Management competencies. The project included entities such as Customer, Restaurant, Menu Item, Order, Order Item, Delivery Driver, Payment, Review, and Delivery Address. I developed the business rules and requirements, created an EER diagram, converted the conceptual model into relational tables, established primary- and foreign-key relationships, and normalized the database.

I then implemented the database in MySQL, populated the tables with sample records, and developed queries involving joins, aggregate functions, `GROUP BY`, `HAVING`, subqueries, and stored procedures. This project demonstrates my ability to progress from a business problem to a functional database rather than merely applying isolated SQL commands.

Additional evidence includes structured prompts, reusable AI workflows, research activities, model comparisons, and generated deliverables that I evaluated and refined. These artifacts demonstrate my ability to transform loosely defined business problems into structured instructions and improve AI outputs through clear context, constraints, validation, and documentation.

I am prepared to apply these competencies to the **Data Management** phase of my capstone project. I will begin by identifying the system’s business rules and data requirements and translating them into an ER/EER model. The identified entities will be converted into relational tables with appropriate primary and foreign keys and normalized through 3NF.

I will implement the database using MySQL and develop queries to retrieve operational and analytical information, including service utilization, appointment patterns, revenue trends, customer activity, inventory levels, and security incidents. These results will support the **Business Analytics** phase of the project.

The GitHub repository will include:

- Business requirements
- Business rules
- ER/EER diagram
- Normalized relational schema
- Data dictionary
- DDL and DML scripts
- Sample data
- SQL queries
- Stored procedures

Together, these materials will demonstrate how Data Management establishes the foundation for the remaining capstone components. Business Analytics will generate insights from the data, Software Systems will deliver application functionality, and Cybersecurity and Networking will protect the system and its information.

Overall, I am competent in foundational and intermediate relational data management and proficient in practical generative AI usage and prompt engineering. My strongest capabilities include requirements-based database design, ER/EER modeling, normalization, MySQL implementation, SQL data retrieval, and reusable workflow design. These competencies support my work as a **Business Analyst** by enabling me to connect business requirements with organizational data, technical systems, analytics, and responsible artificial intelligence.
